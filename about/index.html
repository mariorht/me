About Me

Computer Vision & Software Engineer · 3D Inspection · PhD Candidate

I specialize in bridging the gap between complex 3D vision research and robust industrial production. With over 8 years of experience, my focus is on developing scalable software architectures that integrate AI and 3D sensing to solve high-precision inspection challenges, from hardware integration to frontend applications.

Currently, I am part of the core team leading the new Computer Vision Department at Musashi Spain. Together, we are building the technical foundation from the ground up to automate complex industrial processes.

Simultaneously, I am pursuing a PhD at the University of Oviedo, focusing on: “3D inspection systems for large-volume parts and complex geometries.”

Core Expertise

  • Vision: 3D Metrology, Laser Triangulation, Deep Learning.
  • Software: C++, Python, embedded systems, web/frontend, Linux, Docker.
  • Industrial: Real-time inspection, Quality Control automation, Smart Manufacturing.
